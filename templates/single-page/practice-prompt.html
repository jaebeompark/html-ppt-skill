Turn a pitch deck into a screening note

1 · Summarise the deck

You are an associate at an early-stage VC fund.
Read the attached pitch deck and write a one-page screening note with:
- what the company sells, in one sentence a non-expert understands
- the market it claims, and the number it uses to size it
- traction: every metric stated, with the period it covers
- the three claims that would most change your view if they were false

Quote the deck for every number. If a number is not in the deck, write
"not stated" rather than estimating it.

2 · Pull the questions out

From the screening note above, list the 8 questions you would ask the
founder on a first call. Rank them by how much the answer would move your
decision, not by how easy they are to ask.
