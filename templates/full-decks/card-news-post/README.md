# card-news-post · 9-card carousel

A 3:4 portrait card-news format, 9 cards at 810 × 1080. Structure: cover → hook → pain point → aha moment → steps 1–3 → result → follow CTA.

Handwritten-note and sticker feel with rounded hard shadows, on a warm cream ground with soft pink-orange-yellow gradients. Every card carries an `N / 9` page sticker top-right, and the last one carries hashtags.

**Use when:** Instagram carousels, Threads image posts, blog hero images, 카드뉴스.
**How to use:** screenshot each `.slide` directly, keeping the 810×1080 ratio. Press → to walk through them.
**Feel:** a scrapbook page — stickers, handwriting, a friend passing you something useful.

**Path:** `templates/full-decks/card-news-post/index.html`
