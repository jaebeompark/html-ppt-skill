# card-news-pastel

Warm cream `#fef8f1` ground, blurred colour blobs, big Playfair italic serif display type, and full-colour macaron cards (peach / mint / sky / lilac / lemon / rose).

**Visual traits:** three soft-light blobs in the background, a chip + page combination along the top, Playfair italic for accent words, full-colour 28px rounded cards, italic Playfair numerals 01–04, an SVG donut chart, small gradient divider bars, and serif display mixed with a sans body.

**Use when:** lifestyle, personal growth, light or emotional content; you want a magazine-ish, handmade feel rather than a technical one; themes like rest, slowness, and warmth.
